⚠ One trophy can be missed — read this first

#28 Positive Mental Attitude is the game's only missable trophy: Lucia's balcony meditation in Chapter 1 disappears once that chapter ends, and there is no mission replay. Meditate on the balcony before Chapter 1 ends and keep a Chapter 1 manual save as insurance. Sources: outputlag, keengamer, gtavibes.

Time-sensitive, not missable: the Chapter 3 street-racing strand “Race Against Yago” (hourglass icon) — do it the moment it appears, and save first. It counts toward Strangers & Freaks (#25).

Save strategy (no mission replay, no New Game Plus): 15 manual slots + 1 autosave. Make a manual save at the start of every chapter and keep one untouched save from before the final mission. Never use cheats on your trophy save — cheats disable trophies and saving.

Sources & honesty

The 55 names, descriptions and grades are the PlayStation 5 trophy list as published on 18 Nov 2026 (starter list supplied with this entry, cross-checked against playday.one and outputlag, both updated 18 Nov 2026). Rockstar Games and Sony have not published a standalone official list yet — this guide lists the trophies as reported in the live PSN/Xbox trophy databases on 18–19 Nov 2026, and hidden-trophy methods are still being confirmed by players. Every guide above is written in this entry's own words; sources are named beside each trophy.

Counts are used only where they appear in the official description or are verified by outputlag (18 meditation spots; 42 Shooting Range golds across 2 ranges; 14 drone locations; 9 gangs; 39 illegal operations; 8 garages; 8 bank vaults for 100%; 300 stunts, 70 challenges and 200 vehicles for 100%; 500 actions). Other counts stay unverified and are written without a number. Player rarity percentages are not available at launch — this guide uses difficulty (1–5) and time estimates instead of invented percentages.
